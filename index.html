Why the path looks the way it does

Two layers. You only ever live in one.

where you spend your time

The product layer

Features, data quality, the UI, the words, distribution. The only place ideas compound, and where every answer above is really aimed.

configure once, forget

The platform layer

Hosting, database, auth, payments, email. Wired once with native integrations, then it runs itself. Everything the tool picks lives here so you don't have to think about it again.

The knot feels big because it's the whole platform layer at once. The trick isn't to be a genius about it. It's to make each decision a choice between two or three known options, decide once, and get back to the product.
